04 — FAQCommon questions, clear answers

Frequently asked questions.

What brands usually ask before we start working together on Meta, Google, ChatGPT, Amazon and Shopify ads.

Roshan is a Performance Marketing Specialist based in Wayanad, Kerala, India, with 8 years of experience. He plans and scales paid campaigns on Meta Ads, Google Ads, ChatGPT Ads, Amazon Ads and Shopify for D2C and ecommerce brands in India, the UAE and Canada.

A performance marketing specialist plans, runs and optimises paid ads that are measured by results: sales, leads, ROAS and cost per acquisition. That covers strategy, targeting, creative testing, tracking and budget scaling, so every rupee of ad spend can be traced to revenue.

Most D2C brands need both. Meta Ads create demand by showing your product to people who don't know you yet, while Google Ads capture demand from people already searching. I usually start with Meta for discovery and use Google Search and Shopping to close high-intent buyers.

ChatGPT ads show sponsored placements alongside ChatGPT's answers, so brands reach people while they are actively asking for recommendations. Because the user has already described what they need, intent is high. I run them as a new channel next to Meta and Google, measured on the same sales targets.

Early signals usually show in the first 2 to 3 weeks while campaigns exit the learning phase and creatives are tested. Stable, scalable results typically arrive between month two and month three, once the winning audiences, ads and offers are clear.

It depends on your margins. Many ecommerce brands treat 3x to 4x as healthy, but a high-margin product can be profitable at 2x and a low-margin one may need 5x or more. I set a break-even ROAS from your actual margins first, then build targets above it.

Fix tracking first: Meta Pixel, Conversions API and Google Ads conversion tracking connected to Shopify. Then test creatives and offers on a controlled budget, move spend to the winners, retarget visitors and cart abandoners, and raise budgets gradually so performance holds as you grow.
